2026 Technical Regulations

F1's biggest regulatory reset since the 2014 hybrid era: a new power unit formula, active aerodynamics, a narrower tyre, and a revised prize-money split for the newly-expanded 11-team grid.

The Business of F1 — 2026

A GM's-eye view of the sport's financial guardrails this season.

$215M
Team cost cap for 2026 (≤24-race season), up from $135M in 2023–25 — the jump mostly reflects previously-excluded items (depreciation, more staff costs) moving inside the cap, not new spending headroom.
$190M
Separate power-unit cost cap for 2026, raised to absorb the cost of the new hybrid engine formula and ease the path for new manufacturers.
11 teams
Grid expands from 10 to 11 for 2026: Audi enters as a full works team (built on the former Sauber base) and Cadillac (General Motors) joins as an all-new entrant.
